Kilonaut community rules

Effective 9 November 2026

Kilonaut's Friends and feed let you share your workouts and talk with people you've added as friends. To keep it a good place to train together, everyone agrees to these rules before they post or comment.

The rules

  1. Be kind. No harassment, bullying, threats or hate, in comments, captions, usernames or anywhere else.
  2. Keep it clean. No nudity or sexual content, including in profile pictures.
  3. No spam. No ads, selling, or links to get people somewhere else.
  4. Be yourself. Don't pretend to be someone else, and share only your own training.
  5. Look out for each other. Report anything that breaks these rules: long-press a comment, or tap ⋯ on a post or profile. Reports are anonymous to the person reported.

What happens if someone breaks them

Reports are reviewed by the developer. Content that breaks the rules is removed, and accounts that break them, especially more than once, can be deleted. You can also block anyone at any time: it ends the friendship and stops them from following you or seeing your training.
