Kilonaut Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026

Kilonaut is an Android app (with a Galaxy Watch / Wear OS companion) for logging workouts, made by Vanya Evtushenko. This page explains what the app does with your data. Kilonaut was called GymTracker until November 2026.

In short: Kilonaut works fully without an account, and then your data stays on your phone. If you create a Kilonaut account, your training is also kept in the cloud so it follows you to a new phone, and you can add friends, who see your workouts, streak, best sets and posts. Kilonaut has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sells your data. You can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time. A crash report is sent if the app crashes, with no workout or personal data in it; you can turn that off.

What the app stores

Everything you enter: workouts, exercises, sets, weights and reps, routines and programs, personal records, body weight, notes, settings, and photos you attach to a workout. It is kept in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps can't read.

Your Kilonaut account (optional)

You can create an account with Google ("Continue with Google") or with an email address and password. Accounts are run on Google's Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). With an account, Kilonaut keeps a copy of the following in your account, so it is there on any phone you sign in on:

  • Your workouts (exercises, sets, weights, reps, times, notes on workouts), your body-weight log, your training plan, programs, exercise notes and settings
  • For email accounts: your email address and a password (the password is stored by Firebase in hashed form; nobody, including the developer, can read it). For Google accounts: your Google account's name, email address and profile photo address, as Google provides them

Photos attached to workouts are not uploaded; they stay on your phone. Only you can read your training; this is enforced by the database's security rules, not just by the app. The developer can technically access the database to run the service and investigate reports, and does not look at your training otherwise.

Signing out stops syncing; your training stays on the phone. Google's handling of this data is covered by Firebase's privacy information.

Friends, profiles and posts (optional, with an account)

To use Friends you pick a username. Then:

  • Visible to anyone signed in to Kilonaut (so people can find you): your username, your name (your Google account's name unless you change it), a short bio and your profile picture, if you set them. Your email address is never shown to others.
  • Visible only to your friends (people you follow who follow you back): your number of workouts, how many you did this week and your weekly goal, your weeks-in-a-row streak, your last workout (its name, length, sets and volume), the best sets of your most frequent exercises, and the workouts you share (a summary of a workout you logged, with an optional short caption). Friends can like and comment on your posts and reply to comments, and you on theirs; likes and comments are visible to the post's author and their friends.
  • Never visible to others: your body weight, your workout notes, photos attached to workouts, your email address, your full training history.

Profile pictures are shrunk on your phone (to about 256 pixels) and stored in the database. Posts can't contain photos. You can delete your posts and comments, and comments on your posts, at any time. Removing a friend ends what you can see of each other.

Notifications: when someone sends you a friend request, accepts yours, or likes, comments on or replies to your posts and comments, a small note (who, what, and for comments the first words) is left for you in your account. Your phone checks for notes about every 15 minutes while online, shows them as notifications, and deletes them; a list of recent activity (up to 80 items) is kept on your phone for the Activity page. You can turn these notifications off in Android's settings for Kilonaut (the "Friends" category).

Reports and blocking

Everyone agrees to the community rules before posting or commenting. You can report a person, post or comment, and block people. A report sends the developer: who reported (your account), who is reported, which post or comment, the reason you picked, and the reported text. The person reported is not told who reported them. Reports are read only by the developer, to keep Kilonaut safe, and are deleted once reviewed. Blocking someone ends any friendship, stops them from following you, and hides their comments from you; your list of blocked people is visible only to you.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose "Sign in with Google", the app asks Google for two permissions:

  • Its own app-data folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata). Backups go into a hidden folder that only Kilonaut can use. The app can't see or change any of your other Drive files.
  • Your email address (userinfo.email), only to show which account you're signed in with. It is stored on your phone.

Backups go straight from your phone to your own Google Drive. The developer has no access to them. A backup contains your workout data and settings. Attached photos are not uploaded.

To stop: sign out in the app (Profile → Backups). To delete the backups too: in Google Drive on the web, open Settings → Manage apps → Kilonaut → Options → Delete hidden app data. Google's own handling of your Drive is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Other backups and imports

You can save backups to a folder or file you choose, or import data from Strong or Hevy export files. The app only reads and writes the files and folders you pick.

Android's own device backup may also include the app's data, depending on your phone's backup settings. This is handled by Android and your Google account, not by Kilonaut.

Health Connect (optional)

If you connect Health Connect, Kilonaut can:

  • Write your finished workouts (exercise sessions) and the body weight you log
  • Read body weight, to keep it in sync with the app
  • Read heart rate during a workout (for example from a Galaxy Watch through Samsung Health), to show it in the workout

Data read from Health Connect is only used to show it to you inside the app. It is never shared, sold, sent to the developer, or used for advertising. You choose exactly what to allow, and you can change it at any time in Health Connect's settings.

Galaxy Watch / Wear OS app

The phone and watch apps sync the current workout and the buttons you press (such as finishing a set) directly with each other, using Google Play services on your own devices. This data is not sent to the developer.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, a crash report is sent to the developer through Google's Firebase Crashlytics, so the problem can be found and fixed. A report contains:

  • The error and where in the app's code it happened
  • The phone model, Android version, app version, and the time of the crash
  • A random installation identifier made by Crashlytics, so repeated crashes on one phone can be told apart from crashes on many phones

It does not contain your workouts, body weight, notes, photos, email address or Google account. Reports are used only to fix the app, never for advertising, and are not sold or shared. Google keeps them for up to 90 days; see Firebase's privacy information.

To stop sending them: Profile → Advanced → Send crash reports.

Camera and photos

The photo picker and camera are used only when you choose: to attach a photo to a workout (it stays on your phone), or to set a profile picture (uploaded, as described above). The app only gets the photos you pick.

Sharing

When you tap Share (for example a workout summary image), the app creates the image on your phone and hands it to the app you pick. Nothing goes to other people unless you share it or post it to your friends.

Notifications and alarms

Rest timer notifications, alarms and vibrations are created on your phone. No data is sent anywhere for them.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: Profile → Account → Delete account. After you confirm who you are, it deletes your account and everything in it: your training in the cloud, your stats, username, profile picture, posts (with their likes and comments), your likes and comments on your friends' posts, your friends and follow requests, and your blocked list. You choose whether the training on your phone is kept or deleted too. This happens right away and can't be undone.

Without the app: see how to delete your Kilonaut account, or email evtushenkovanya16@gmail.com from the address of your account. Requests are handled within 30 days.

Comments you left on posts of people who are no longer your friends can't be reached from the app; email to have them deleted. Reports you filed are kept until they are reviewed. Without an account, uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings deletes everything stored on your phone (Profile → Advanced → Delete all data does the same). Google Drive backups can be deleted as described above. Health Connect data can be managed or deleted in Health Connect.

Children

Kilonaut is not directed at children under 13, and you must be 13 or older to create an account or use Friends and posts. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email the developer and it will be deleted.
