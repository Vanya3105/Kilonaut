Delete your Kilonaut account

Kilonaut, by Vanya Evtushenko

Deleting your Kilonaut account removes the account and everything in it. It can't be undone.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Kilonaut and go to Profile.
  2. Tap your account at the top, then Delete account.
  3. Choose whether to keep the training on your phone, then confirm it's you (your password, or your Google account).

Your account and its data are deleted right away.

Without the app

Email evtushenkovanya16@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Kilonaut account", from the email address of your account (for Google sign-in, your Google account's address). Include your username if you have one. Your account is deleted within 30 days, and you get a reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your account (sign-in)
  • Your training kept in the cloud: workouts, body-weight log, training plan, programs, notes and settings
  • Your username, name and profile picture
  • Your posts with their likes and comments, and your likes and comments on others' posts
  • Your friends, follow requests and blocked list, and the stats your friends could see

What is kept

  • Reports you filed about others are kept until they are reviewed, then deleted.
  • The training on your phone is kept if you choose that in the app; uninstall the app or use Profile → Advanced → Delete all data to remove it.
  • Google Drive backups you made are in your own Google Drive; delete them there (see the privacy policy).

More about your data: Kilonaut Privacy Policy.
